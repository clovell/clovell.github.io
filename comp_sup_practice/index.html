Finding the Base

To form comparatives and superlatives, you must first isolate the base of the positive adjective. For 1st/2nd declension adjectives, remove the -a or -us. For 3rd declension, remove the -is from the genitive singular.

The Comparative Degree

Formula: Base + -ior (M/F) or -ius (N) + 3rd Declension Endings.

Example: longus, -a, -um (Base: long-) → longior, longius.

The Superlative Degree

Formula: Base + -issim- + 1st/2nd Declension Endings.

Example: long- → longissimus, -a, -um.

Note: Adjectives ending in -er or -lis may use -rimus or -limus.
